    Actual exported image

    Shown at its actual pixel size. Scroll to inspect. Guides are never included in the downloaded photo.

    Three simple points.

    1. Top of head: use the crown definition shown for your country. Some authorities measure the natural crown; others include hair.
    2. Bottom of chin: click the lowest central point of the chin, where the face ends and the neck begins.
    3. Between eyes: click midway between the pupils. This sets the horizontal face centre. It also helps you see if the face is tilted.

    Click “Help me mark” for one step at a time. You can drag a coloured marker to correct it, or use the numeric fields. Then click “Fit using my markings”.

    The helper fits only the original pixels. It cannot fix a turned face, closed eyes, bad lighting, missing shoulders, the wrong background or an inadequate source photo.
